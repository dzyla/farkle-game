How to Play Farkle

  • Goal: Be the first to reach the target score.
  • Roll all 6 dice. Select dice that form scoring combinations.
  • Scoring Dice:
    • Single 1 = 100 pts
    • Single 5 = 50 pts
    • Three 1s = 1000 pts
    • Three 2s = 200 pts
    • Three 3s = 300 pts
    • Three 4s = 400 pts
    • Three 5s = 500 pts
    • Three 6s = 600 pts
    • 4 of a Kind = 1000 pts
    • 5 of a Kind = 2000 pts
    • 6 of a Kind = 3000 pts
    • 1-6 Straight = 1500 pts
    • 1-5 Straight = 750 pts
    • 2-6 Straight = 750 pts
    • Three Pairs = 1500 pts
    • Two Triplets = 2500 pts
  • You MUST select at least one scoring die after each roll.
  • After selecting, you can either:
    • **Bank:** Add round points to total score & end turn. (Any valid score can be banked).
    • **Roll Again:** Roll remaining dice for more points.
  • **Farkle:** No scoring dice on a roll? Lose round points & turn ends.
  • **Hot Dice:** All 6 dice score? Get bonus points & roll all 6 again!
